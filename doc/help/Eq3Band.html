Eq3Band

The 3 band EQ is a treble and bass equalizer with sweepable Mid frequency plus gain controls for the Mid band and for the fixed 80Hz and 12kHz frequency bands. It has also a master level control.

Lo Gain

With the Lo gain knob you change the gain at 80Hz. Range -18 to +18 dB.

Mid Gain

With the Mid Gain knob you change the gain at the Mid frequency (see below). Range -18 to +18 dB.

Mid Freq

With the Mid Freq control you change the center frequency of the Mid band. Range: 100Hz to 8.00 kHz.

Hi Gain

With the Hi gain knob you change the gain at 12kHz. Range -18 to +18 dB.

Lvl

With the Lvl knob you attenuate the input signal [Attenuator Type I].

See also Common Filter parameters.
