MinMax

The MinMax module compares two input signals and outputs the highest levels on the Max output and the lowest levels on the Min output. This module is originally named a ‘level switcher’ on old analog systems. The MinMax module can be used to ‘sort’ two signals. It can be used to ‘split’ two lfo signals into two new signals where one signal is always at a lower value and the other signal always at a higher value. The modula can also be put to good use in the audio range to ‘mix’ two unisono detuned waveforms like triangle waves. See also Common Level module parameters.
