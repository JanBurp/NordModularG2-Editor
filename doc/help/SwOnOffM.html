SwOnOffM

The Momentary OnOff Switch is perfect for manual triggering of different things in the Patch - especially when you assign the On button to an Assignable Button on the synth panel. When you click the On button, the switch "closes" for as long as you hold the button and then automatically "opens" when you release the button. If no signal is patched to the input, the output will send the value 64 units when the switch closes. See also Common Switch parameters.
