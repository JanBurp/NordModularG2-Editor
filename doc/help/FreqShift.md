# FreqShift (Frequency Shifter)

The Frequency Shifter module shifts the frequency content of the audio signal up or down. Shifts all frequency components by a fixed amount, creating unusual harmonic effects.

## Parameters (alphabetical)

### Mix

Sets the dry/wet mix. Range: 0-100%.

### Shift

Set the frequency shift amount. Range: -5000 to +5000 Hz.

## Inputs

### In1

Audio input.

## Outputs

### Out1

Processed audio output.
