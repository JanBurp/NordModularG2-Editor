# FltHP (Non-Resonant Highpass Filter)

A highpass filter with selectable slope and cut-off frequency modulation. The passband is flat with unity gain.

Note: Changing slope will force the Sound Engine to recalculate and thus cause a brief moment of silence.

## Parameters (alphabetical)

### FREQ KNOB

Sets the initial cut-off frequency.

### KBT SCROLL BUTTON

Keyboard tracking toggle. When enabled, the cut-off frequency tracks the played note pitch.

### dB/OCT RADIO BUTTONS

Selectable slope: 6, 12, 18, 24, 30, or 36 dB/Oct.

### ON/BYPASS

Enables or disables the module.

## Inputs

### IN

Audio input.

### Mod

Modulation input for cut-off frequency.

### PITCH MODULATION

Modulation input for pitch-based cut-off frequency modulation.

## Outputs

### OUT

Audio output.
