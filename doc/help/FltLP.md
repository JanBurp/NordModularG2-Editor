# FltLP

Non-resonant lowpass filter with selectable slope (6/12/18/24/30/36 dB/Oct) and cut-off frequency modulation input. Passband is flat with unity gain.

## Parameters

### FREQ KNOB - Cut-off frequency control.
### dB/OCT - Slope selection (6/12/18/24/30/36 dB/Oct).
### KBT - Keyboard tracking (1V/octave cut-off modulation from pitch input).
### FreqMod - Cut-off frequency modulation amount from Pitch input.
### ON/BYPASS - Enables or bypasses the filter.

## Inputs

### In - Audio input.
### Pitch - Cut-off frequency modulation input (1V/octave).

## Outputs

### Out - Filtered audio output.
