# EqPeak - Parametric equalizer module with center frequency, gain, and bandwidth control.

## FREQ Knob

Sets the center frequency of the equalizer. Range: 20 Hz to 16.1 kHz.

## GAIN Knob

Sets the gain at the center frequency. Range: -18 to +18 dB.

## BW Knob

Sets the bandwidth around the center frequency. Range: 2 to 0.02 octaves.

## LEVEL Knob

Attenuates the input signal. (Attenuator Type I)
