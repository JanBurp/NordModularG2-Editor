# X-Fade

Crossfade mixer with control signal modulation.

**LOG/LIN** — Switch between Linear and Logarithmic characteristics for the 1/2 knob. 'Lin' is suitable for control signals and 'Log' for audio signals. In 'Log' mode both input signals have an attenuation of about -3dB when the 1/2 knob is in center. In 'Lin' mode both input signals have an attenuation of -6dB at center (mathematical mean of inputs 1 and 2 divided by two).

**X-FADE MODULATION INPUT** — DYNAMIC CONTROL/AUDIO modulation input. Connect a modulator here. The amount of modulation is controlled with the ATTENUATOR TYPE I knob.

**1/2 KNOB** — Sets the initial mix of the two signals. Click the triangle to set equal amount of both signals.

Click to select linear Pan characteristics instead of logarithmic.
