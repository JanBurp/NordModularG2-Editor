# Eq3Band - 3-band equalizer with sweepable mid and level control

## Parameters

**LO GAIN** — Gain at 80Hz. Range: -18 to +18 dB.

**MID GAIN** — Gain at the mid frequency. Range: -18 to +18 dB.

**MID FREQ** — Center frequency of the mid band. Range: 100Hz to 8.00 kHz.

**HI GAIN** — Gain at 12kHz. Range: -18 to +18 dB.

**LVL** — Master level control [ATTENUATOR TYPE I].

## Description

A treble and bass equalizer with sweepable mid frequency plus gain controls for the mid band and for the fixed 80Hz and 12kHz frequency bands. Includes a master level control.
