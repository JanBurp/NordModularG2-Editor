Eq2Band

The 2 band EQ is a treble and bass equalizer with gain controls for 80Hz and 12kHz plus a master level control.

Lvl

With the Lvl knob you attenuate the input signal [Attenuator Type I].

Lo Gain

With the Lo gain knob you change the gain at 80Hz. Range -18 to +18 dB.

Hi Gain

With the Hi gain knob you change the gain at 12kHz. Range -18 to +18 dB. See also Common Filter parameters.
