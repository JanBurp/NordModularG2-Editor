# Eq2Band (2 Band Equalizer)

A treble and bass equalizer with gain controls for 80Hz and 12kHz plus a master level control.

## Parameters

### GAIN Knob

Changes the gain at the center frequency. Range: -18 to +18 dB.

### BW Knob

Sets the bandwidth around the center frequency. Range: 2 to 0.02 octaves.

### LO GAIN Knob

Changes the gain at 80Hz. Range: -18 to +18 dB.

### HI GAIN Knob

Changes the gain at 12kHz. Range: -18 to +18 dB.

### LVL Knob

Attenuates the input signal.

## Inputs

### INPUT

Audio signal to be equalized.

## Outputs

### OUTPUT

Equalized audio signal.
